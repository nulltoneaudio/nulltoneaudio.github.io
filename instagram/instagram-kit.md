## 4. Reels — six concepts

1. **"This is the low end"** — 15s. Kick and sub layer solo'd, then the full mix. Caption the frequency, not the feeling.
2. **"Cue point by ear"** — 20s. Scrubbing a track, finding the drop, dropping the cue exactly on the transient. No talking.
3. **"One take"** — 30s. A single filter sweep over an unreleased track, hands only, no face.
4. **"What I deleted"** — 15s. Tracks cut from a set, names redacted, reason in text. Shows taste, not just output.
5. **"Load in / load out"** — 30s. Time-lapse of setting up the booth and tearing it down, ending on the mark.
6. **"The room at 3am"** — 20s. Crowd from behind the booth, one light, the drop unmixed and live.

Format rules: 9:16, text in the top third and bottom third only (UI eats the rest), captions burned in, no music you don't own.

## 5. Hashtags

Instagram rewards relevance over volume — use **5 to 8**, never 30.

**Core set (use on most posts)**
`#nulltone #djmix #rekordbox #ddjflx4 #harmonicmixing #bedroomproducer #djlife`

**Genre sets — pick one that matches the actual track**

- House / minimal: `#housemusic #minimalhouse #deephouse #techhouse`
- Techno: `#techno #dubtechno #hardgroove #peaktime`
- Breaks / bass: `#breakbeat #bassmusic #dnb #jungle`
- Open format: `#openformat #partydj #clubdj`

> Genre is the one thing I couldn't infer from your GitHub — pick your lane and stay in it in the first month, or the algorithm won't know who to show you to.

## 6. Cadence and formats

**Three posts a week, minimum:**

- **Monday — process.** Studio detail, a version comparison, a rig shot. Low production, high credibility.
- **Wednesday — music.** A clip, a release, a mix excerpt. The thing you want to be known for.
- **Saturday — the room.** Show footage, crowd, flyer, recap. Proof you actually play.

**Stories daily** (5 minutes): one studio detail, one track you're into, one repost of a post. Use the highlight covers to keep them organised.

**DMs to bookers, template:**
