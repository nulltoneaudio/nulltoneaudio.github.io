NULLTONE — icon concepts / 01–10

Pick a head.

Each card shows the mark large and again at 22px, which is roughly how it reads in a notification or a favicon. Reply with a number, or a hybrid like "02 body with 07 crest".

01

Cabinet

Square speaker cabinet, horn ears, twin driver eyes, waveform mouth. The most character of the set.

02

Cyclops

Same cabinet body, one large driver eye and a slot mouth. Simplest, strongest silhouette.

03

Cassette

Tape body, reel eyes, window mouth. Instantly music, but reads retro rather than bass.

04

Vinyl

Grooves, label eye, small tonearm. Beautiful large, but an object rather than a face.

05

Jack plug

Quarter-inch plug as a body: tip antenna, collar, tiny face. The most unusual silhouette.

06

Headphones

Band and cups over a round head. Reads DJ fastest, but is the least ownable.

07

Mohawk

A waveform crest for hair, visor eye, slot mouth. Punk signal energy.

08

Boombox

Handle ears, twin speaker eyes, slot mouth. Nostalgic and very legible.

09

Pad grid

Sampler pads arranged as a face. Producer-coded, collapses into dots when small.

10

Microphone

Vintage mic capsule, grille rings, stand. Clean and classic, least expressive.
