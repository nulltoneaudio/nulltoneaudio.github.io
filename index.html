About

Ethan Banker, California.

Portrait · 4:5

I make the music I want to hear at 3am.

I'm Ethan Banker, and I record and play as NULLTONE. I came up mixing by ear — matching keys, riding the low end, and learning that the best part of a set is usually the thing you leave out. That's still the whole method.

The records are built the same way: start from silence, add only what earns its place. Bass-heavy, deliberate, and made to be played on a system with headroom rather than a phone speaker.
