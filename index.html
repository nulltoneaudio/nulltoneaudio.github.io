03

About

Two obsessions, one signal path.

Portrait · 4:5

Most DJs learn the software. I kept opening it up.

I'm Ethan Banker — a DJ and producer in California. I got tired of building sets by dragging tracks around a list, so I wrote Parity: it reads a rekordbox library directly and shapes a program around the energy curve I want. I got tired of one controller talking to one app, so I wrote Cuewire and put the FLX4 in charge of the visuals too.

The music and the code come from the same place: I want the low end right, and I want to understand the machine that gets it there.
