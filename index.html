03 — About

I make music for the last hour of the night.

I'm Ethan Banker. I record and play as NULLTONE, out of California.

I came up mixing by ear — matching keys, riding the low end, learning that the best part of a set is usually the thing you leave out. The records are built the same way: start from silence, add only what earns its place.

Bass-heavy, deliberate, made for a system with headroom.
